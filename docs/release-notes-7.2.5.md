# LILLA Audio Sampler 7.2.5

## Import complete audio files into Flash

RAW, WAV, AIFF and MP3 files are now imported with their complete decoded mono audio, within Flash capacity. The former 3 MiB limit (approximately 35.7 seconds) no longer truncates longer files.

In Sound Edit, select the passage you want by moving the playable window along the entire file. Its maximum length is determined by the per-source PSRAM capacity.

Files previously imported with truncation must be re-imported from their complete originals to make the remaining audio available.
